# Climate-Change-Project

#This is a webpage made to demonstrate the decision making process for a farmer in Kansas who is choosing which crop to plant based on changing conditions. The external factors are climate warming levels, drought prevalence, insurance costs, and water availability in Kansas. The website shows based on the initial conditions whether planting high yield high drought risk wheat is more profitable than lower yield lower drought risk sorghum.
